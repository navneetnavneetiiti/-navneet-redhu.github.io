01 / Profile

My work explores the structure and construction of frames on locally compact abelian groups, with applications to sampling, signal processing, and information recovery.

I am interested in problems that connect abstract harmonic analysis with explicit constructions and numerical implementation.

02 / Research

Questions about
representation and recovery.

01

Applied harmonic analysis

Fourier analysis and operator methods for understanding structured signals and information.

02

Frame theory on LCA groups

Parseval frames, cyclic frames, and explicit constructions in locally compact abelian settings.

03

Sampling and phase retrieval

Sampling geometry, dynamical sampling, and the recovery of signals from incomplete measurements.

04

Wavelets and applications

Wavelet frames, shearlets, and connections to data science, diffraction, and signal processing.
